# Camera Traps in New Places
Upload photos from **one** camera trap and compare species predictions before / after test-time adaptation
(no labels used). CSE-414 project. Models: supervised ResNet-50 and self-supervised DINO ViT-S/16, trained on a
subset of iWildCam (WILDS).
